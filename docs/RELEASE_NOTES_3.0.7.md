**Rooms you can change after they open, and a clearer word when a turn's messages are spent.**

## What changed

- **Rename a room, change its agents, or start again with the same team.** A room's More menu now has Rename, Agents and New room with these agents, and a room can be renamed from its right-click menu in the sidebar. Agents adds or removes agents, two to eight, and says who leads when the lead is taken out. New room with these agents opens a new room with the same agents, lead and settings, and leaves the old one and its conversation as they are. Changing a room now follows the same rules as making one: a name is required, and a room holds two to eight agents, each counted once. Thanks @aprlx21 (#370)
- **A spent turn says so.** When a turn's twelve changes and its one kept-back message are used, a later message to another agent is refused in words that say the kept-back one was already delivered, that nothing was sent this time, and where it can go instead. It no longer reads as a send still in progress. Thanks @alexnayko (#369)
