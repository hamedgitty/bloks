**A side panel rebuilt around tabs and windows: minimize, expand, a sharper browser with viewports, meeting notes beside the chat, emoji in agent names, and connected apps for Antigravity agents.**

## What changed

- **The side panel opens on a list.** The panel button opens it on the right. With nothing open there it lists every panel it can show, each with what it holds now: lines added and removed in green and red, and how many are running in the browser, the terminal or background tasks. Closing the last tab brings the list back. Tabs are pills with their icon and name, the + sits right after them, and a new page or shell joins the pane that already holds the browser or the terminal. Thanks @iamvince (#522)
- **Minimize, expand and close.** Each pane's top row ends with minimize, expand and its own close. Minimize turns a tab into a small window over the conversation that you can drag to any corner, where it springs into place, two at most; its header comes up on hover with the way back and an X that keeps it as a tab for next time. Rest the pointer on expand for its menu: move the tab (or all its tabs) to any side, expand, or open it in a window of its own. Once expanded it reads Show conversation, and in a chat its buttons take the top row's place so nothing shows twice.
- **The browser.** Pages are sharp on a sharp screen, moving ones included, and each tab shows its site's icon. A cleaner toolbar: back, forward, reload and the address, then extensions, the servers the agent started, and one menu with open in your browser, copy link, save screenshot, viewport, downloads, only the agent's tabs, browser settings and clear browsing data. Viewports lay pages out as a phone, a tablet or a laptop screen, fitted in the panel; the minimized browser shows the page and takes its shape. The browser also opens in a window of its own with its tabs.
- **Meeting notes in the side panel.** It moved out of the header into the side panel, with a notes icon, and keeps listening while another tab is in front. The chat header is now just the side panel button.
- **A quieter header.** A room keeps view as topics and the side panel; sharing, the folder, a call, renaming, agents and the rest are in a small menu after the room's name. An agent's name has the same menu, with its settings, a call when it has a voice, a new conversation and clearing this one.
- **The conversation tab in front** carries the sidebar edge's colours, faintly, up its side and round its corner.
- **Tooltips** on every button of the side panel, the browser and the minimized windows.
- **Color emoji in agent names show everywhere.** The app's fonts now end with the system's color emoji fonts, so a name that starts with an emoji no longer shows a blank where some systems had no fallback. Thanks @pycckuu (#545)
- **Antigravity agents get their connected apps and MCP servers.** A turn that has either runs through `agy acp`, which takes them per session: exactly what the agent's settings grant, keys and headers kept off the command line, and anything agy asks about comes to you as an approval card. agy 1.5.14 takes stdio servers there but not HTTP ones, and a server it can't take stops the turn and says why. Turns without apps or servers run as before. Thanks @pycckuu (#546)
