**Bloks knows an Android phone when it sees one.**

## Fixes

- **A member who joins from Android shows as Android.** The Android app says it is Android when someone joins a workspace with it, but Bloks only knew iPhones, Macs, PCs and browsers, so the Members page and the join request card called that person's phone an iPhone. They now say Android, and "on an Android phone" on the request card.
- **Phone words fit any phone.** Pairing, location and place watchers said "your iPhone" wherever they meant the phone: "Pair your iPhone", "Paused on your iPhone", "when your iPhone says you arrived". They now say "your phone", in all seven languages, and so do the notes your agents read about where you are. With the Android app on Google Play, both phones pair, share location and answer approvals the same way.
