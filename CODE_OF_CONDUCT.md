# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be someone people want to build with. Assume good faith, argue about the
work rather than the person, and take a correction the way you would want
one given to you.

## What that means here

Expected:

- Give feedback on code, not on the person who wrote it.
- Say what you actually think, plainly, and be willing to be wrong.
- Ask before assuming someone acted in bad faith.
- Remember that most contributors are doing this in time they did not
  have to give.

Not accepted:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in any venue connected to
  this project.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption: derailing threads, reopening settled decisions to
  wear people down, or dogpiling.

This applies in issues, pull requests, commit messages, code comments,
and anywhere someone is representing the project.

## Reporting

If something crosses the line, email the maintainer at
hamedr@outlook.com. Reports stay private. If the report is about the
maintainer, say so and it will be handled by someone else.

Include what happened, where, and roughly when. You do not need to have
a perfectly documented case to raise something.

## What happens next

Maintainers will read the report, ask questions if the picture is
unclear, and decide. Depending on severity that ranges from a private
word, to a public correction, to a temporary or permanent ban from the
project. Whoever reported it will be told the outcome.

Maintainers who do not hold themselves to this are subject to the same
consequences as anyone else.
